ABOUT ME

I connect how things look with how they work.

I'm Hemil, a product and visual designer based in Edmonton. My background started in development, which taught me to think about structure, logic and how digital experiences actually work.

Over time, I moved deeper into design because I wanted to shape more than functionality. I wanted to create experiences that feel clear, intuitive and visually memorable.

Today, I work across product design, UI/UX and visual design. I enjoy understanding the problem first, simplifying complexity and turning ideas into experiences that feel intentional from the first interaction to the last.

Curiosity first. Clarity always.
